How it works

One Mac at the front of the room. The speaker changes nothing.

1

Speak

A mic on the speaker. Audio streams to Tencent 实时语音翻译 and each sentence comes back translated the moment it lands.

2

Show

A Display window on the projector, a transparent Overlay on your slides, or a QR code for phones. Portrait strip, landscape bar, or text over slides.

3

Keep

Recording never touches the network. Afterwards: clean subtitles from the cloud, an MP4 with the text burned in, a PDF summary.

Where it helps

Anywhere following the words is the difference between attending and taking part.

Conferences and talks会议・讲座

The venue screen, the slides and every phone in the audience — from one laptop.

Classes and workshops课堂・工作坊

Students follow on their own screens. The recording and summary become the notes.

Meetings会议室

Cantonese and Mandarin speakers in one room, reading the same line.

Video and audio files视频・录音

Drop a file in the web app: subtitles in minutes. Edit the cues, export SRT, VTT, text or MP4.

Made for the room

Every part of this was shaped by running it at real talks.

1Overlay on any displayA transparent window over your slides, on any screen including virtual ones, from the 字幕 menu bar item.
2Phones follow alongA link and a QR code. Attendees pick translation, original or both, and their own text size. Audio never leaves the Mac.
3Recording that cannot dropMP3 and subtitles are written locally as the talk runs. A lost connection pauses subtitles, never the recording.
4Re-subtitle via cloudAfterwards, run the whole recording through in one pass and fill any gap the connection left.
5GlossaryNames, places and terms the recogniser should favour. Kept with your account, shared by the app and the web.
6Learning summaryA concise summary with clickable timestamps, as Markdown or an A4 PDF.

Files, too

Upload video or audio, up to five hours. Pick the spoken and subtitle languages; cues come back split at punctuation, 22 characters a line, timed to the speech.
